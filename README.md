### 🔬 About Me
I am a **Doctoral Researcher** at Miyazaki University, specializing in computational electromagnetics and high-frequency electromagnetic field analysis. My primary mission is developing scalable, high-performance solutions for complex physics simulations.

### 🚀 Core Focus
My research heavily involves building **multi-node MPI parallel processing environments** across Linux clusters and benchmarking sparse linear systems for **GPU solver acceleration**. Beyond software, my engineering work spans developing 3D nodal FEM algorithms, configuring cross-platform networks, and performing thermal maintenance on high-performance hardware.
